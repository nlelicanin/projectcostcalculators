How this ecommerce website cost calculator works

The estimator uses the platform approach as its baseline, then adjusts for catalog size, custom design, integrations, migration effort, international requirements, and provider type. The goal is to separate a straightforward online store from a commerce project that behaves more like a software integration program.

What belongs in an ecommerce development estimate

A useful ecommerce quote is more than “design + checkout.” Commerce projects combine content, product data, payments, shipping, tax configuration, customer accounts, analytics, and operational integrations. The most expensive surprises usually appear where the storefront has to exchange data with another system.

  • Storefront: theme/templates, navigation, product pages, search/filtering, cart, checkout configuration.
  • Catalog: products, variants, collections/categories, media, attributes, inventory rules, and bulk imports.
  • Operations: shipping, fulfillment, tax, returns, customer notifications, and staff workflows.
  • Integrations: ERP, PIM, accounting, CRM, email/SMS, marketplaces, reviews, subscriptions, or custom APIs.
  • Migration: products, customers, redirects, order history, content, tracking, and QA.
  • Launch: payment testing, transactional email, analytics, conversion tracking, device testing, and rollback planning.

Shopify's current ecommerce cost guide similarly separates platform, design, apps, and ongoing expenses. If your store handles card payments, payment architecture and provider responsibilities should also be scoped with the PCI Security Standards Council requirements in mind.

Build cost vs. ongoing ecommerce cost

The result above is a build/implementation estimate. Do not compare it with a platform's monthly subscription price as if they measure the same thing. After launch, an ecommerce operation can also have recurring platform or hosting fees, paid applications, payment processing, email/SMS, fraud prevention, search/recommendation tools, support, and development retainers.

Ecommerce quote checklist

Use these questions before asking providers to quote. They force the important assumptions into the open.

  • Which platform and hosting responsibilities are included?
  • How many products, variants, collections, price lists, and customer groups are involved?
  • Who cleans and maps product/customer data before import?
  • Which shipping, tax, accounting, CRM, ERP, PIM, subscription, and marketplace systems must connect?
  • Are existing URLs being preserved, and who owns redirect mapping during a migration?
  • What payment, refund, order, email, analytics, and device/browser tests are part of acceptance?
  • What is excluded from the one-time build and becomes an ongoing subscription or support cost?

Ecommerce Website Cost Calculator: common questions

How much does an ecommerce website cost?

Ecommerce website cost varies with platform approach, catalog size, design requirements, integrations, migration work, and provider type. A small templated store can be far less expensive than a custom or headless build connected to ERP, fulfillment, subscriptions, or international commerce systems.

What costs are not included in the build estimate?

The calculator focuses on one-time implementation. Recurring platform subscriptions, payment-processing fees, paid apps, hosting, email/SMS services, fraud tools, and ongoing maintenance should be budgeted separately.

Is Shopify always cheaper than custom ecommerce development?

Not necessarily. A hosted platform can reduce infrastructure and baseline development work, especially for standard stores. Heavy customization, complex integrations, unusual checkout requirements, or headless architecture can make implementation substantially more expensive.

How does product count affect ecommerce development cost?

Catalog size matters most when it creates migration, data-cleanup, variant, merchandising, search, filtering, or integration work. Adding 5,000 clean products through an automated import can be easier than manually rebuilding 200 inconsistent products.

Should an ecommerce redesign use this page or the redesign calculator?

Use this page when commerce functionality is the main cost driver. Use the Website Redesign Cost Calculator when the main question is preserving and migrating an existing site, especially if URL, CMS, and SEO migration work dominates the project.
