Privacy Policy

We built Project Cost Calculators to be privacy-first. Calculator inputs run locally in your browser and are not stored or transmitted to us.

Effective date: 2026-01-10

Summary

  • No calculator inputs are stored. Calculations run locally in your browser.
  • No accounts. We do not require sign-ups.
  • Limited analytics. We use privacy-friendly Google Analytics settings to understand overall usage.
  • Email contact. If you email us, we receive the information you send.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect or store the values you enter into calculators (such as pricing inputs, budgets, or project details). We also do not offer user accounts and do not ask for passwords or payment information.

Information we may collect

Like most websites, we may receive basic technical information when you visit, such as your browser type, device type, approximate location (city/region), and the pages you view. This data is used in aggregate to understand site usage and improve content.

We configure analytics with privacy-friendly settings (e.g., IP anonymization and limited signals). We do not attempt to identify you.

Cookies and tracking

We use Google Analytics 4 in a privacy-friendly configuration (e.g., IP anonymization, no ads personalization signals, and no client-side storage where possible). We use it to understand aggregated usage (like page views), not to build advertising profiles. Depending on your browser/device, limited analytics storage may still occur.

If we add advertising in the future, we will update this policy and (where required) provide additional notices or controls.

Email

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and any information you include in your message. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information.

Third-party links

This site may link to third-party websites. We do not control their content or privacy practices. Please review their policies separately.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise this page.

FAQ

Do you store any calculator inputs?

No. Calculator inputs are processed locally in your browser and are not stored or transmitted to us.

Do you use cookies?

We configure analytics in a privacy-friendly way and do not require cookie consent banners by default. Depending on your browser and settings, some limited analytics data may still be collected.

What data is collected when I visit the site?

We may collect limited, aggregated usage data (such as page views) to understand which calculators are helpful and to improve the site. We do not collect the values you enter into calculators.

How can I contact you about privacy?

Email support@projectcostcalculators.com with your privacy question.
