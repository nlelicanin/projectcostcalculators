About Project Cost Calculators

Project Cost Calculators is a set of privacy-first calculators that help you plan budgets and pricing ranges using practical, real-world cost drivers.

What this site is

This site is designed for quick, realistic planning. Each calculator focuses on the handful of inputs that usually move price the most — scope, complexity, integrations, timeline, and provider type.

Use the results to sanity-check a budget, compare options, or prepare better questions when getting quotes.

Who this is for

  • Buyers: sanity-check budgets before requesting proposals.
  • Founders: compare website vs SaaS build ranges and plan milestones.
  • Freelancers: translate hours into fixed-price packages using buffers and margins.
  • Teams: align stakeholders on scope and tradeoffs (speed vs quality vs cost).

What these estimates are (and aren’t)

The numbers you see are planning ranges, not quotes. Real pricing varies by vendor, region, quality level, and what’s included (strategy, design, content, ongoing support, etc.).

If your project has unusual requirements (custom integrations, strict compliance, migrations, legacy systems), expect the “real” range to move.

How the estimates are calculated (simple methodology)

Each calculator starts with a baseline range for the service type, then adjusts the range using a small set of inputs that typically change real-world quotes: scope, complexity, integrations, timeline urgency, and provider type.

The intent is to produce a planning range that’s conservative enough to be useful, while still reflecting the fact that different providers price risk, overhead, and quality differently.

To understand the building blocks behind quotes, see the Service Cost Breakdown.

Privacy-first by default

Calculations run locally in your browser. Inputs are not stored and are not transmitted.

FAQ

Are these estimates exact quotes?

No. The calculators provide planning ranges based on common pricing drivers. Real quotes vary by vendor, scope, and requirements.

Do you store any data I enter?

No. Calculations run locally in your browser and inputs are not stored or transmitted.

How are the calculators built?

Each calculator uses transparent inputs and conservative multipliers to produce a realistic range. The goal is to help you plan a budget and ask better questions when getting quotes.

Can I use these calculators for my business pricing?

Yes—use them as a starting point. Adjust inputs to match your real utilization, overhead, scope assumptions, and target margins.
