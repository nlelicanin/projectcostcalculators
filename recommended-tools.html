Best for tracking scope, timelines, deliverables, and stakeholder approvals.

  • ClickUp — strong all-in-one project system for teams.
  • Monday.com — flexible work management for agencies and operations.
  • Notion — docs, briefs, requirements, and lightweight planning.

Best for freelancers and service businesses: pricing, contracts, invoices, and retainers.

  • FreshBooks — invoicing and accounting basics for service businesses.

Recommended tools: common questions

Are these tools required to use the calculators?

No. The calculators work without any external tools. This page lists optional tools that can help with scoping, quoting, delivery, and ongoing operations.

Do you use affiliate links?

Some links may be affiliate links, meaning we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We recommend tools based on usefulness for service pricing and delivery.

Which tools are best for freelancers?

Freelancers typically benefit most from proposals/invoicing tools and a lightweight project system. Bonsai, FreshBooks, HoneyBook, and Notion are common starting points.

Which tools are best for SaaS projects?

SaaS projects often need strong project tracking and reliable hosting. ClickUp or Monday.com plus DigitalOcean or Cloudways are common choices depending on team size and ops maturity.
